# Key Insights and Recommendations

Figures are taken from the Power BI dashboard. Update them if the segment counts change
after the frequency scoring fix.

## Champions
- 1,109 customers (26% of the total) generate about 66% of revenue.
- Average recency is about 13 days, and average frequency is about 10 orders.
- **Action:** loyalty rewards, early access to new products, and review requests.

## At Risk
- 657 customers (15% of the total) have an average recency of about 149 days.
- They have averaged about 3.4 orders each, so they have real past value.
- **Action:** re-engagement emails with a clear reason to return, such as new product
  alerts or a limited-time offer.

## Loyal Customers
- 834 customers (19%) generate about 16% of revenue.
- **Action:** upselling, bundle offers, and loyalty programs to raise average order value.

## Repeat Purchases
- The repeat customer rate is 65%, so about one third of customers never return after
  their first order.
- **Action:** a second-purchase offer sent within 30 days of a first order.

## Geography
- The United Kingdom generates about £7.2M of the £8.74M total, roughly 82% of revenue.
- The Netherlands, Ireland, and Germany are the next largest markets, each well under 5%.
- **Action:** test targeted marketing in the Netherlands, Ireland, and Germany.

## Seasonality
- Revenue is highest in November, which matches the pre-holiday gift-buying season.
- The December 2011 figure is incomplete because the data ends on 9 December.
- **Action:** plan stock and campaigns ahead of October.

## Product Concentration
- One bulk order of 80,995 units inflates the revenue of PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE.
- **Action:** use the Pareto chart to identify the products that drive most revenue,
  and review bulk-order pricing separately.
